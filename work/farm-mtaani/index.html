Farm Mtaani connects buyers and agricultural sellers, combining product discovery, seller-facing workflows and operational administration in a single marketplace experience.

01 / CORE STORY

The challenge

A marketplace is more than a product grid. Buyers need clear browsing and purchasing paths; sellers need ways to manage what they offer; and admins need visibility into the health of the system.

Orders and payment-related transitions must remain consistent even as different users interact with the same marketplace.

02 / CORE STORY

Product experience

The user-facing experience includes products and seller journeys, catalogue discovery, order flows and a set of administrative tools.

The admin interface supports operational monitoring and reliability work. The product has been developed iteratively with attention to preserving existing customer and payment workflows.

03 / CORE STORY

Implementation approach

I treated the marketplace as a system of connected states rather than independent pages: catalogue, users, seller actions, order lifecycle and administration all need to agree.

Security and regression tests protect important data and workflows as the interface evolves.

04 / CORE STORY

Current status

Farm Mtaani remains an evolving product. This case study intentionally avoids invented figures about users, revenue or commercial adoption.
