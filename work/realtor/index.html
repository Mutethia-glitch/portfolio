An architectural-plan and property marketplace concept with an approved design baseline and a staged technical roadmap for growing it into a real service.

01 / CORE STORY

The challenge

People exploring architectural home plans need visually clear listings, plan details and obvious ways to compare options and move toward a purchase.

Behind the interface, customer accounts, admin management and digital order handling require careful sequencing.

02 / CORE STORY

Design work

The approved prototype explores architecture and plan listing cards, a detail experience and a premium storefront presentation.

The screenshot is from the Home Harmony Hub visual design concept, not a launched production Realtor site.

03 / CORE STORY

System roadmap

The Realtor repository documents sequential tasks, acceptance criteria and a disciplined flow of implement, validate, review and progress.

Security, admin permissions and payment workflows are considered in the architecture before the platform is represented as production ready.

04 / CORE STORY

Next steps

The next phase is translating approved prototypes into a secure, testable customer and admin system, without presenting planned capabilities as already delivered.
