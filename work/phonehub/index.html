PhoneHub is a phone and electronics storefront moving from design prototype toward a secure catalogue and permissioned customer experience.

01 / CORE STORY

The challenge

A phone store needs convincing product discovery without pretending to be a real merchant before the infrastructure is ready.

The more important engineering work is making sure user sessions, personal cart data, and privileged admin actions are scoped appropriately.

02 / CORE STORY

What is implemented

The development branch reads published product data from client-owned Supabase when configured. Email OTP sign-in is integrated, customer cart records are protected with row-level security, and the admin area uses role checks.

The current frontend prototype is shown in the image above; it is not a photograph of the secured backend development build.

03 / CORE STORY

Responsible scope

No real checkout or payments are enabled. A verified merchant account, webhooks and end-to-end payment testing are necessary before orders or card transactions could be offered.

Avoiding simulated sales in the live product gives the demonstration a clear line between working functionality and features still under development.

04 / CORE STORY

What comes next

Persisted authenticated sessions, client-owned catalogue configuration, integration checks and payment verification are future milestones — not claims of already shipped features.
